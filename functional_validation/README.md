# functional_validation

Supporting analysis for manuscript revision. Three lines of evidence assembled on the same 131-sample cohort (28 colorectal cancer patients, 103 healthy controls after FASTQ quality control):

1. **Direct functional gene profiling via HUMAnN3 v3.9.** Shotgun reads classified against UniRef90 protein database without a taxonomy step, then regrouped to enzyme commission (EC) numbers and MetaCyc pathway abundance.

2. **Metagenome-assembled genome (MAG) quality and taxonomy.** All 746 assembled bins evaluated using CheckM2 v1.1.0. 226 retained at medium-quality threshold (>70% complete, <10% contamination), of which 103 reached high-quality. GTDB-Tk v2.7.2 (reference release r232) classified 225 of 226 MAGs to species level (99.6% resolution).

3. **AGORA community model concordance.** Per-patient AGORA community metabolic models compared against HUMAnN3-detected gene content. Concordance measured pre-FBA (structural EC presence) and post-FBA (Spearman correlation against predicted flux under simulated diets).

All analyses performed on PARAM Smriti HPC at NABI Mohali.

## Sample-flow audit

- Original cohort: 134 patients (28 CRC + 106 Healthy)
- 3 Healthy samples excluded due to FASTQ data integrity issues (SRR5898944, SRR5898948, SRR5898949)
- HUMAnN3 processed: 131 samples (28 CRC + 103 Healthy)
- AGORA-comparison cohort: 129 samples (26 CRC + 103 Healthy) — 2 CRC patients lacked AGORA model files (di_p16, di_p24)

## Headline findings

### Pre-FBA concordance (n=129)
- Mean Jaccard: 0.333
- 50.9% of AGORA-predicted ECs supported by HUMAnN3
- Per-metabolite concordance: 100% for acetate, butyrate, succinate, putrescine across both cohorts; 96-100% for propionate; partial for H2S (46-84%) and TMA (41-77%)

### Post-FBA Spearman correlation
- Diet-dependent patterns observed across 35 (diet × metabolite) combinations
- TMA shows strong diet-dependent reversal: ρ = -0.459 under HFD (p = 4.7e-08), positive under VEGAN/PROTEIN/GLUTENFREE
- Acetate similarly diet-dependent: ρ = -0.408 under GLUTENFREE, +0.231 under VEGAN

### MAGs
- 226 quality-filtered MAGs from 111 of 131 patients (85% per-patient coverage)
- 99.6% species-level taxonomic resolution
- 103 high-quality MAGs (≥90% complete, <5% contamination)

## Reproducibility

Scripts have hard-coded paths to PARAM Smriti HPC. To re-run elsewhere, edit path constants at the top of each script. Steps execute in numbered order. Large intermediate files (HUMAnN3 EC matrices, per-sample full data) are excluded from this folder due to size; they can be regenerated by running the pipeline.
